# Step & Style — Client Confirmation Questions

Please answer only the business decisions and ownership questions below. Day-to-day store values should be entered by the client through the admin panel rather than sent to the developer.

The current products and categories are demonstration data. They will be removed before final handover, and the client will add and manage the real catalog from the admin panel.

The client can already manage products, categories, inventory, variants, product and campaign discounts, promo codes, shipping rates, bank-transfer and mobile-wallet details, official contact information, social links, shipping/return/privacy/Terms policy content, homepage content, blogs, reels, reviews, newsletter presentation, and analytics IDs from admin.

## Admin-managed information

The following admin controls are not currently available and need to be implemented before the client can manage them without developer assistance:

- Affiliate commission, attribution, payout, and public-program rules
- Courier name, shipment tracking number, and customer tracking display
- Abandoned-cart delay and message content

The client should enter these values directly after their admin controls are available; the values are not requested in this document.
